ABOUT AETL

Advocacy for Ethical & Transformative Leadership

AETL is an independent platform for thoughtful reflection, ethical conversation and transformative ideas on leadership, faith, institutions and society.

Our purpose

AETL exists to create space for thoughtful and responsible engagement with the questions that shape leadership, faith, institutions and society. It encourages reflection that moves beyond criticism toward moral responsibility, practical wisdom and constructive change.

Our approach

AETL welcomes intellectual honesty, thoughtful questioning and constructive criticism. It encourages engagement with difficult issues without losing sight of human dignity, moral responsibility and the possibilities for change. Its Opinion Papers are editorial publications, not peer-reviewed academic articles. They are assessed for relevance, clarity, originality and responsible argument.

Publication identity

Each publication forms part of the AETL Opinion Paper Series and is identified by its title, author and publication date. Papers may be cited by their assigned number, such as AETL Opinion Paper No. 001. The series provides a continuing space for independent reflection on ethical leadership, faith, institutions and society.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are AETL Opinion Papers?

AETL Opinion Papers are reflective pieces that engage contemporary ethical, leadership, religious, and societal issues. They provide a space for informed perspectives and constructive conversation.

Are AETL Opinion Papers academic or peer-reviewed?

No. AETL Opinion Papers are not academic or peer-reviewed publications. They are editorial and reflective pieces intended to stimulate thoughtful discussion and public engagement.

How are submissions reviewed before publication?

Submissions undergo editorial review for relevance, clarity, originality, responsible argument, and alignment with AETL’s purpose and values. Editorial review is distinct from academic peer review.

What topics does AETL publish?

AETL publishes reflections on ethical leadership, church and society, religious coexistence, moral responsibility, governance, integrity, peacebuilding, and social transformation, among related issues.

Can I contribute an Opinion Paper to AETL?

Yes. AETL welcomes well-considered contributions that engage meaningfully with ethical, leadership, religious, and societal issues. Contributions should be clearly written, responsibly argued, and respectful of differing perspectives.

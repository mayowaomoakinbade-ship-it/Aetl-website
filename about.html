ABOUT AETL

Advocacy for Ethical & Transformative Leadership

AETL is an independent personal publishing and advocacy initiative devoted to the promotion of ethical, accountable and transformative leadership.

Our purpose

AETL exists to create a credible space for carefully reasoned opinion papers that engage contemporary questions of leadership, Christian ethics, governance, institutional culture and social transformation.

Our approach

The platform values intellectual honesty, constructive criticism, moral responsibility and practical relevance. AETL papers are opinion and advocacy publications; they should not be represented as peer-reviewed journal articles unless independently subjected to such a process.

Publication identity

Publications may be identified as AETL Opinion Paper No. 001, No. 002, and so forth. Each paper carries its title, author, publication date and citation information.
